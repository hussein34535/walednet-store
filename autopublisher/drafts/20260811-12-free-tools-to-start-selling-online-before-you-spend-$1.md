# 12 Free Tools to Start Selling Online Before You Spend $1

The biggest barrier to starting an online business isn't capital; it’s friction. You feel you need a website, a payment processor, and a fancy logo. The truth is, the internet already provides everything you need to accept money today. You just have to know where to look.

Here are 12 practical, free tools to launch a digital storefront, handle payments, and deliver products without writing a single line of code or spending a dime.

## 1. Square for Digital Products
You don't need Stripe for every customer. If you want to sell a single PDF or a discount code, Square is superior. It offers a free digital checkout link. You upload your file, they handle the secure delivery, and you get a payment notification. It charges a flat processing fee per transaction but requires no monthly subscription or setup fees.

## 2. Gumroad for Simplicity
Gumroad is often criticized for its high fees on large sales, but for starting out, it is unmatched in utility. You can list a PDF, a Notion template, or a music pack. They provide a hosted page for free. You can connect your PayPal or Stripe account to take payments. It is the fastest way to go from "idea" to "sale" with zero setup time.

## 3. Buy Me a Coffee (for Recurring Revenue)
If you are selling a service or a recurring course, Buy Me a Coffee is better than Stripe. It allows you to set up a subscription button. You don't need to build a membership site. You can offer a "Coffee" tier where customers get access to a private Discord or a weekly newsletter. It charges a small flat fee on top of the payment processor's cut, but the recurring revenue model is built-in.

## 4. ConvertKit (Free Tier)
Do not build an email list on social media. Social platforms are renting you the land; email is buying it. ConvertKit’s free tier allows you to have up to 1,000 subscribers and automations. When you sell a product via Gumroad or Stripe, use their landing pages or email sequences to onboard new buyers immediately. This builds a repeatable asset.

## 5. Stripe (Standard Checkout)
For selling high-ticket items or subscriptions, Stripe is the industry standard. While they charge 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction, they do not charge monthly fees for the basic account. Use their "Checkout" feature, which is a hosted page. You don't need to build a custom form; Stripe handles the PCI compliance and security. This is essential if you plan to sell SaaS or software.

## 6. GitHub (for Code and Assets)
If you are selling code, software, or digital assets, host them on GitHub. It is free, highly secure, and widely trusted. You can create a private repository for your paid products and use a tool like **Buy Me a Coffee** to grant access only after payment is received. GitHub also provides download links that can be easily integrated into any landing page.

## 7. Notion (for Digital Products)
Notion templates are the most popular low-cost digital product right now. You don't need to build a custom HTML site. You simply make a public page in Notion, export it as a PDF, and sell the file. However, you can also use Notion to build the storefront itself. It is a robust database that can manage inventory, customer names, and file delivery.

## 8. Canva (for Visuals)
Your product needs a face. You do not need to hire a designer. Canva’s free tier is powerful enough to create professional thumbnails, landing page headers, and social media graphics. A good image converts better than a bad image, and doing it yourself keeps your margins high.

## 9. Raycast / Spotlight (for Quick Access)
When you are selling, you need to be fast. Raycast (for Mac) and Spotlight allow you to search your files instantly. If you sell templates or assets, you need to locate the file to email it or generate a download link without getting lost in your folders. It increases your operational speed significantly.

## 10. Ghost.org (for Blogs and Newsletters)
If you plan to sell knowledge as a service, Ghost is the best free option. Unlike WordPress, Ghost is built specifically for writers and creators. It allows you to build a membership site where you can charge for access to articles. It handles the subscription and password protection out of the box.

## 11. Framer (for Landing Pages)
You can build a portfolio or product page on Framer using their free plan. It allows for custom animations and a very professional look without writing CSS. Framer connects easily with Stripe for payments. It is the best "no-code" tool for creating a visual brand presence that doesn't look like a generic Bootstrap template.

## 12. HeyGen (for Video)
Video is a premium product. You can use HeyGen's free tier to generate AI avatars that present your course or product pitch. You don't need to be on camera. Using video assets allows you to sell a premium experience even if you are just selling a PDF guide.
